# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Investors and funds (primary audience of the brand).** They put capital into real estate and judge NOVA by rigour, clarity of numbers and whether a step will pay off. They meet NOVA through LinkedIn, a proposal PDF, an Investment Intelligence report, a digital business card shown in a meeting, an email signature.
- **The NOVA team (users of Compass).** A small team of partners, architects and consultants using the Compass app daily on desktop, iPad and phone: projects and phases, hours, agenda, proposals (Pipeline), land opportunities (Investment Intelligence), finances, Brandbook.

## Product Purpose

NOVA Associates helps clients find investment solutions in real estate, and helps owners get more out of assets they already hold, through the way it thinks numbers and does projects. It is a partner from A to Z: it reads the market, guides the client rather than waiting for instructions, and looks for the most agile, light, authentic and solid investment vehicle. Done well, for investors and for buyers, a satisfied client comes back; that is how legacy is built for investors.

Compass is NOVA's internal operating app and the home of the Brandbook, which hands the team every brand asset (wallpapers, LinkedIn, post generator, email signature, digital card).

## Positioning

Investment partners and consultants with architecture in-house, not architects waiting for a brief. The difference is how numbers and project are thought together (land reading, PDM constraints, viability, ROI) and the guidance from first look to licence.

## Operating Context

- Proposal PDFs (fee proposals, PT/EN), meeting minutes, Investment Intelligence PDF reports, client account statements: all generated from the app.
- LinkedIn company page (cover, Life tab, posts) and personal profile covers; wallpapers for laptops and phones; Teams meetings; Outlook email.
- Single-file web app (index.html) on GitHub Pages with Supabase; brand drawing shared in assets/brand/nova-draw.js.

## Brand Commitments

- **Logo:** the NOVA wordmark and the N monogram stay exactly as they are (confirmed by the user, 2026-10-14).
- **Tagline:** "Creating Impact. Driving Returns. Building Legacy." (current; open to proposal).
- **Voice:** dependable, solid, honest, and written with appeal, but never self-praising, never presumptuous, never sounding AI-written. Say what NOVA does, not how good it is.
- **Terms kept as-is:** Lead time, Work time, Score, Partner, Compass, Mission Control, "valores de referência".
- **Languages:** Portuguese (Portugal) first, English second.

## Evidence on Hand

Very little yet. No case studies, testimonials, team photography or press to show. Numbers in brand material must be marked as examples unless the user supplies them. Never invent clients, returns, volumes or awards.

## Product Principles

1. Guide, don't wait: every surface should show the next right step.
2. Numbers carry the argument; adjectives don't.
3. Lightness of process: fewer, surer steps.
4. Solid before showy: trust is earned by precision and consistency.
5. Understatement: let the work speak; no boasting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Team uses the app on phones in the field and in meetings: text ≥ 12 px in the app, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, light and dark modes.
